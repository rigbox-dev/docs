---
title: "Stop a local tunnel"
openapi: "/openapi/tunnels-api.json DELETE /api/v1/tunnels/{id}"
playground: "none"
---

<Info>
These endpoints manage HTTPS tunnels under `rigbox.link`. Use the signed CLI
0.13.1 or later to run the local connector. Read the
[exposure and shared-browser-site risks](/guides/local-tunnels#shared-browser-site)
before sharing an app.
</Info>

Stops the owned tunnel, revokes its connector and lease credentials, and invalidates its viewer sessions. The relay closes active traffic after observing the stopped state.

Stop keeps the tunnel record in your history. Use [Delete tunnel history](/api-reference/tunnels/delete-record) to remove an inactive record.

A stopped connection generation cannot be resumed. Start a new tunnel through the [CLI](/guides/local-tunnels#start-privately) when you want to serve the website again.
