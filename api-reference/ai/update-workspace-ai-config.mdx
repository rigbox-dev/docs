---
title: Update Workspace AI Mode
openapi: "/openapi/rigbox-api.json PUT /api/v1/workspaces/{id}/mode"
description: Set a workspace's AI mode (managed or BYOK).
---

Accepts `mode` — `"managed"`, `"byok"`, or `null` to clear the per-workspace override and fall back to your account default. Provider and model are account-level; set them with [Update AI Defaults](/api-reference/ai/update-defaults). For BYOK, supply your provider key to the workspace as an environment variable (e.g. `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`).
