## Source and coverage

The command tree comes from the CLI's Clap definitions at revision `09bd402`, including both local and workspace modes. Hidden internal commands and Clap's automatically generated `help` routing aliases are excluded; every documented command supports `--help`.

Syntax and flags are generated independently of maintained examples. Examples containing uppercase placeholders require your values. Commands that create, change, or delete resources perform those operations when run; `--help` is always offline.
